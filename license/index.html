Licence and reuse

Short version: take it, use it for anything, tell no one.

Unless a work says otherwise, everything published here is released into the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal.

What that means

You can copy, modify, translate, recolour, redraw, animate, print, embed, sell, and redistribute these files. In any medium. For any purpose, commercial or not. Alone or as part of something larger. You can relicense your version however you like, including under terms stricter than these.

You do not need permission. You do not need to give credit. You do not need to keep a notice, link back, or state that anything changed. You do not need to tell anyone, and there is no one to ask.

If a credit line is useful to you anyway, use whatever wording suits you. It is welcome and it is not required.

What CC0 actually is

CC0 is not a licence with conditions. It is a waiver: the author gives up copyright and related rights worldwide, as completely as the law allows. Where a country does not permit an author to waive those rights, CC0 falls back to an unconditional, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to do anything, forever.

Nothing is more permissive than this. It is the ceiling.

The limits that remain

A few things sit outside any author's power to give away, so they are worth naming plainly. They are not conditions imposed here:

  • other people's trademarks and patents are untouched by this waiver
  • the publicity and privacy rights of anyone depicted are unaffected
  • no warranty of any kind is offered, and no liability accepted
