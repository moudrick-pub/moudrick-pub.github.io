Licence and reuse

Short version: use it for anything, including commercially, and say where it came from.

Unless a work says otherwise, everything published here is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

What you may do

  • use the files in presentations, courses, documentation, articles, books, video
  • use them commercially, including in paid products and paid training
  • modify them: recolour, crop, relabel, redraw, translate, animate
  • redistribute the originals or your modified versions

No permission request is needed and no fee is due.

What you must do

Give credit. A credit needs four things: the title of the work, the name of the author, the licence, and a link back to the source. Each work page has that line written out in plain text, HTML and Markdown, ready to copy.

For example:

"Plan first" by moudrick, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Source: https://moudrick-pub.github.io/works/devops-infinity-loop-min/#plan-first

If you changed the file, say so briefly. "Recoloured from the original" or "labels translated" is enough.

Where the credit goes is up to you and the medium: a caption under the image, a credits slide at the end of a deck, an image-credits section in an article. It just has to be reasonably findable by someone looking at the work.

What you may not do

  • drop the credit
  • add restrictions of your own on top, or apply DRM that stops others exercising the same rights
  • imply that the author endorses you or your product
